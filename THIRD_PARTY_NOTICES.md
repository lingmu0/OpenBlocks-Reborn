# OpenBlocks Reborn redux

Based on Noped01/OpenBlocks-Reborn-redux, branch 1.21.1, revision f4265a2,
and its upstream lingmu0/OpenBlocks-Reborn. Existing author attribution is retained.

The legacy guide excerpts and sound icon textures are from OpenBlocks 1.12.2-1.8.1,
copyright OpenMods Team, under the MIT license reproduced in LICENSE-OpenBlocks.
Guide excerpts are marked as historical reference; current implementation notes
describe the modern port separately.

The complete common and NeoForge implementation and resources of ElevatorMod
1.21.1-1.11.4 by VsnGamer and contributors are incorporated under MIT; see
LICENSE-ElevatorMod and docs/elevator-import.json. Packages, IDs and integration
entry points are adapted to OpenBlocks. No separate ElevatorMod mod is registered.

Patchouli (Vazkii and contributors) remains a separate required dependency and
is not bundled.
